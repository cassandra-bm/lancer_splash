Here's a track I made a few years ago I really like. I'll be posting more in the future! I remember shoving my fingers in my ears as a kid and repeeatedly opening and shutting the little skin flap the covers the sound hole. It turned the noisy environment around me into a weird but fascinating cacophony of sliced up voices and ambient noise. I oscillated the synth on this track and added ambient noise to the backtground to try to recreate that feeling - it felt important to me for some reason.
